BANK SYNC

Bank sync without handing over your bank login

On iPhone, Lens reads Wallet-connected accounts through Apple's FinanceKit, on the device. The transactions are never sent to Lens or to any third-party aggregator, because the reading happens on your phone. Balance anchoring and duplicate detection keep the numbers honest, and pull to refresh brings in the latest. Bank sync is a Pro feature.

Before you rely on it: bank sync requires an iPhone with iOS 17.4 or later (UK connected cards need 18.4 or later), cards connected in Apple Wallet, and it is not available in every country. Everywhere else, CSV import is free on every tier and covers the same ground with one monthly export from your bank.

PRIVACY

How private is Lens?

Lens keeps your finances in a local database on your device, and there is no Lens cloud database of your money. There is nothing to sign up for, so there is nothing to log in to and no profile of you to build.

  • No account, no sign-up, no login.
  • No ads. Your data is not sold.
  • No advertising identifier and no tracking across apps.
  • Anonymous product analytics, on by default, switchable off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and PIN lock. Local backup and restore.

The one exception

If you use the optional AI features, your question and a financial snapshot are sent to Google Gemini to answer it. The request goes through a Lens Cloud Function that holds the key server-side. AI never runs unless you ask it something.

FAQ

The things people ask before they download.

Does Lens need my bank login? add

No, Lens never asks for your bank login or credentials. On iPhone, optional bank sync reads Wallet-connected accounts through Apple's FinanceKit, on the device, so nothing is shared with Lens or any third-party aggregator. It requires iOS 17.4 or later (UK connected cards 18.4 or later), cards connected in Apple Wallet, and is not available in every country.

Does Lens work on Android? add

Yes, Lens is available for both iPhone and Android. Bank sync uses Apple's FinanceKit and is iPhone only. On Android, the free CSV import with bank-format detection, quick manual entry and automatic categorisation cover the same ground.

Is my data encrypted? add

Your data is stored in a local database on your device, and Lens keeps no cloud database of your finances. The app can be locked with Face ID, Touch ID or a PIN. If you use the optional AI features, your question and a financial snapshot are sent to Google Gemini to answer it.

What does the AI see? add

If you ask the optional AI a question, your question and a snapshot of your financial data are sent to Google Gemini to answer it. AI is a Pro feature metered at 100 credits a month, and requests go through a Lens Cloud Function that holds the key server-side. If you never use AI, no AI request is ever made.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro? add

Your data stays on your device and Lens keeps working on the free tier. The free limits apply again: 3 accounts, 3 goals and 5 category budgets, with unlimited recurring bills, the whole daily number, This month reports, net worth and CSV import all still included.

Can I export my data? add

Yes, Lens exports your transactions to CSV with Pro, and local backup and restore is built in on every tier. CSV import is free on every tier as well.

What does Lens cost? add

Lens is free forever, and Lens Pro starts from £4.99 a month with a 7-day free trial. Yearly and lifetime options are available, priced in your own currency in the store.

Which countries does bank sync work in? add

Bank sync uses Apple's FinanceKit and is only available where Apple supports it, on iPhone with iOS 17.4 or later. UK connected cards need iOS 18.4 or later, and cards must be connected in Apple Wallet. Everywhere else, the free CSV import and manual entry work fully.
